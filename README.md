### 🚀 Featured Project — [Menufy AI](https://menufyai.com)

> **Scan any menu, order with confidence.**

Menufy is an AI-powered mobile app that lets you point your camera at any restaurant menu — printed, handwritten, or on a screen — and instantly get every dish translated with name, description, ingredients, and price. No QR code needed, no internet waiter required.

**What it does:**

- 📷 **Point & Scan** — works with paper menus, chalkboards, screens, or gallery photos
- 🌍 **Instant Translation** — AI detects the language and translates every dish into your native tongue. Supports 50+ languages
- ⚡ **Real-time Streaming** — dishes appear one by one as AI processes the image, no waiting for the full result
- 🛒 **Build Your Order** — tap dishes to add them, adjust quantities, see a running total live
- 💱 **Currency Conversion** — prices shown in both the restaurant's and your home currency, rates updated automatically
- 📋 **Scan History** — every scanned menu is saved with date and location, shareable with friends
- 🧾 **Show the Waiter** — one tap shows your full order in the restaurant's language

**Built with:** React Native · Expo · Supabase Edge Functions · Gemini AI · Next.js (landing)
